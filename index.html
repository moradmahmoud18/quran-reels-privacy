Quran Reels Manager - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Quran Reels Manager is an Android application designed to help users manage video content from Telegram and publish it to Facebook Pages that they own or are authorized to manage. We respect your privacy and aim to protect your information.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, the application may access:

  • Telegram account authentication information required to connect to Telegram.
  • Telegram channel information and video content you select.
  • Facebook account and Page information made available through Meta's authorized APIs.
  • Facebook Page access tokens required to perform publishing actions.
  • Captions, hashtags, publishing records, and application settings.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the application's features, including connecting to Telegram, downloading selected videos, managing publishing tasks, and publishing content to Facebook Pages at the user's request.

4. Data Storage and Security

Application data is stored locally on the user's Android device. Sensitive credentials and authentication information are protected using Android security mechanisms. The application does not operate a separate cloud server to store users' Telegram sessions or Facebook Page access tokens.

5. Third-Party Services

The application communicates with Telegram and Meta/Facebook services through their APIs. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Telegram Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing

The application developer does not sell users' personal information. Information is transmitted to Telegram or Meta as necessary to provide the features requested by the user.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can stop using the application at any time. Application data stored locally can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. Users may also revoke the application's access through the relevant Telegram or Facebook account settings.

For assistance with a data deletion request, contact the developer using the email address below. Please include the application name and describe the data deletion request. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to be directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
